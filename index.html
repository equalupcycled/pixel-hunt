HOW TO PLAY

  • TAP a bird to shoot it.
  • Your gun starts with 1 SHELL. Tap RELOAD (or swipe down) — you can't shoot while reloading.
  • Miss 3 targets in a row (birds or deer get away) and it's GAME OVER. A hit resets the counter.
  • Hit streaks multiply your score. GOLDEN BIRDS pay big.
  • PINK LOVEBIRDS are protected — shooting one costs cash. Letting them go is fine.
  • From round 4, DEER dash along the treeline. Worth a lot — and they count as a miss if they escape.
  • WEAPONS: tap to fire the shotgun, handgun & rifle (the rifle pierces in a line from you). Hold to spray the machine gun or sweep the laser (VENT it when hot). Bow: hold to draw, release — arrows arc, so lead your target.
  • TURRETS help with birds about to escape (limited shots). FLAK: tap it, then tap the sky. CALL lures golden birds. DECOY gathers & slows birds.
  • Every 10th round is a BOSS. Shoot it down before its timer runs out — if it escapes it counts as a miss.
  • Unlock MAPS with tougher animals and bigger payouts. Top 10 scores per map go on the LEADERBOARD.
  • Your $ and upgrades are saved forever. Big upgrades take a few runs to afford — keep hunting!
  • Complete missions, unlock achievements and claim your daily reward for bonus cash.
