PRIVACY POLICY

Pixel Hunt · Cache Brands LLC · Last updated Nov 14, 2026

This policy describes how Cache Brands LLC ("we", "us") handles information when you play Pixel Hunt on the web or in a future app build.

No account required

You can play Pixel Hunt without creating an account or signing in. We do not ask for a login, password, or profile.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps the following in your browser's or app's local storage (on your device only). It is not uploaded to our servers today:

  • Game saves: money, upgrades, equipment, map clears, checkpoints, missions, achievements, and settings (including Sound and Music preferences).
  • Scores: your high scores, including separate Classic and Infinity bests per map.
  • A local leaderboard of top scores on this device, which may include a player name you type (up to 12 characters) when a run qualifies.

If you type a name for the leaderboard, it stays on this device unless you later choose to submit scores to an optional online leaderboard (not enabled at this time).

Advertising

Pixel Hunt may show placeholder ads on the web today, and may later show ads through Google AdMob (in an app) and/or Google AdSense (on the web). Those services may collect or use an advertising identifier, approximate location or device information, and data about ad views and clicks, including for personalized ads where allowed. Where required, we or our partners will request consent (for example through a consent message or an OS-level tracking prompt) before using personalized advertising. You can often reset or limit advertising identifiers in your device settings, and you may see non-personalized ads if you decline consent. Rewarded ads (for example, watch an ad to continue a run or double earnings) are optional; you can keep playing without watching them, though you will not receive that reward.

Sharing

The game can share a score through the device's Web Share sheet or a Facebook sharer link, along with a score card image and a link to the game. We do not receive the list of people you share with. Facebook and any other app you choose to share to have their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We do not currently use a third-party analytics SDK or service in Pixel Hunt. If that changes, we will update this policy.

Children (COPPA)

Pixel Hunt is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below and we will delete it.

How to delete data

Because save data, scores, and leaderboard names live on your device, you can delete them by clearing this site's or app's data (for example, clearing site data or cookies for this origin in your browser, or deleting the app and its local data). That removes your save, settings, and local scores from that device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
