HOW TO PLAY

  • TAP a bird to shoot it.
  • Your gun starts with 1 SHELL. Tap RELOAD (or swipe down) — you can't shoot while reloading.
  • Every target that gets away is 1 X. 3 X total and it's GAME OVER (Lucky Feather allows 4, then 5). A PERFECT round gives one X back.
  • CHECKPOINTS every 5 rounds (5, 10, 15...) are saved per map. After game over you can continue from your checkpoint (score starts at 0, X's cleared) — checkpoint runs earn cash but aren't ranked.
  • Or WATCH AD TO CONTINUE once per run: you restart the same round and keep your score (X's cleared). Fresh runs (from round 1) stay ranked after a continue.
  • Beat that map's ROUND 30 BOSS to CLEAR it and unlock INFINITY — endless rounds with a separate Infinity high score per map.
  • In INFINITY you start over with the free shotgun and a separate Infinity wallet. Upgrades there have no level cap and never change your Classic money or upgrades. One Infinity progression is shared across every Infinity map.
  • Hit streaks multiply your score. GOLDEN BIRDS pay big.
  • PINK LOVEBIRDS are protected — shooting one costs cash. Letting them go is fine.
  • Birds fly in from the LEFT and leave on the RIGHT. Each one that gets across = 1 X.
  • From round 4, DEER dash along the treeline. Worth a lot — and they count as a miss if they escape.
  • WEAPONS: tap to fire the shotgun, handgun & rifle (the rifle pierces in a line from you). Hold to spray the machine gun or sweep the laser (VENT it when hot). Bow: hold to draw, release — arrows arc, so lead your target.
  • TURRETS help with birds about to escape (limited shots). FLAK: tap it, then tap the sky. CALL lures golden birds. DECOY gathers & slows birds.
  • Every 10th round is a BOSS (Classic and Infinity). It flies back and forth across the upper sky and drops EGGS. Smash an egg before it hatches for a few points; a hatched bird then flies left to right and counts as 1 X if it escapes. Shoot the boss down before the timer runs out — if it leaves, that is also 1 X.
  • Unlock MAPS with tougher animals and bigger payouts. Top 10 scores per map go on the LEADERBOARD.
  • PERKS in the Upgrades shop are late-game, expensive bonuses that actually change how a run plays: Auto Reload, Speed Loader, Reserve Ammo, Combo Keeper, Critical Shot, Egg Sniper, Cash Magnet, Second Chance, Deer Whisperer, and Bullet Time. They work with every gun (Auto Reload vents the laser and nocks the bow). In Infinity they keep leveling with no cap. Active effects show on the HUD (AUTO, BULLET TIME, SAVES).
  • Your $ and upgrades are saved forever. Buy them in UPGRADES from the title screen or after game over — you can't shop during a run or between rounds. Big upgrades take a few runs to afford.
  • Complete missions, unlock achievements and claim your daily reward for bonus cash.
